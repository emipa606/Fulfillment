# About


Have you ever had that feeling where you don't feel fulfilled because you haven't done something you're really passionate about for a while? Well now your colonists feel the same way! Introducing Fulfillment! Now they have longer-lasting feelings about whether they have been able to do what they really enjoy instead of instantly forgetting about it the moment they are done!

![Image](https://i.postimg.cc/yx4PBPDs/wEcP3eZ.png)

## Features:


### Dynamic Need System:

**Fulfillment:** Colonists will now have a "Fulfillment" need, influencing their mood based on if they have done passionate tasks recently or not.

### Dynamic Rate Changes:

**Passion and Burning Passion:** The rate at which your colonists gain this need depends on how passionate they are about the task they are doing.

### Balanced Fulfillment Rates:

**Dynamic Adjustments:** The need drops and gains at rates designed to ensure balanced gameplay.

## Notes:


- Fulfillment decreases naturally over time, with the rate accelerating at higher levels.
- Animals and mechanoids are excluded from having this need.

## Compatibility:


This mod is designed to be compatible with existing saves and should integrate smoothly with other mods. Ensure that you load this mod after core gameplay mods for the best experience.
